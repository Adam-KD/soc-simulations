\# Simulation Report — RDP Brute Force Attack \& Detection



\*\*Date:\*\* 2026-05-13  

\*\*Attacker Machine:\*\* Kali Linux (192.168.2.10)  

\*\*Victim Machine:\*\* Windows 10 (192.168.1.10)  

\*\*Objective:\*\* Simulating an RDP brute force attack as a follow-up to the reconnaissance phase conducted in Simulation 01, where port 3389 was identified as a potential attack vector. The goal is to evaluate Wazuh's detection capability against authentication-based attacks, understand the SOC analyst's investigation workflow, and document the complete attack-detect-respond cycle.  

\*\*Environment State:\*\* RDP enabled on Windows 10, Windows Defender Firewall configured to allow RDP traffic on port 3389.
